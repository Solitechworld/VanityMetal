# Third-party data

## Bitcoin Puzzle catalog

`Sources/VanityMetalCore/Resources/BitcoinPuzzleCatalog.json` contains the
Bitcoin Puzzle catalog's puzzle number, P2PKH address, catalog status, and
inclusive private-key range. It is derived from the catalog snapshot dated
2026-10-02 published at <https://lotterypuzzle.org/data/>.

The catalog compilation is licensed under CC BY 4.0. Attribution:

> lotterypuzzle (2026). Bitcoin Puzzle catalog, snapshot of 2026-10-02.
> https://lotterypuzzle.org/data/

The status is snapshot metadata and may be out of date; it is not a live
blockchain check.
